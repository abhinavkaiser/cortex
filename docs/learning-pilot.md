# Learning pilot protocol: lesson-order-pilot-v1

Question: does initially presenting practice before explanation improve delayed
application compared with initially presenting explanation? Both groups receive
the same content, tasks and feedback and can switch tabs. Assignment therefore
measures the initial presentation's effect, not restricted access to instruction.

Personal evidence remains available without pilot participation. The Outcomes page
collects optional consent; opting out excludes the learner from cohort aggregates.
Staff and demo accounts are excluded. Assignment is stable per learner. Instrument,
project, curriculum and presentation versions are saved with each check.

Record the original six-item baseline before the project, transfer after all three
current-version milestones pass, and retention at least seven days after transfer.
Keep initial scores and attrition. A failed transfer requires a new passing final
milestone revision before a distinct reassessment; it never rewrites the original
transfer outcome. Use engagement minutes and revisions as descriptive context,
not as proof of learning.

Primary outcome: delayed-check mean, using each participant's mean across domains.
Report enrolled, baseline, transfer and retention counts and missing follow-up for
each arm. Suppress means below five participants; seek at least 20 retention
participants in each arm before reviewing the preliminary comparison. That target
is operational, not a formal power calculation. Avoid causal claims from small or
selectively completed samples, and do not equate project certificates with external
professional accreditation.

Before expanding the study, have subject experts review item difficulty and parallel
forms, compare a sample of rubric grades with blinded human review, inspect errors
and appeals, and assess accessibility with learners. A failed supported-work control
or unsafe/incorrect-work pass pauses automation. Deployment and browser tests cannot
establish educational effectiveness. Real learners and elapsed follow-up time are
required; no simulated data may be used as observed impact.
